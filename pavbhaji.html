Pav Bhaji

Pav Bhaji is a popular Indian street food dish that originated in Mumbai. It consists of a spicy vegetable mash (bhaji) served with buttered and toasted bread rolls (pav). The bhaji is made by cooking a mixture of vegetables like potatoes, peas, tomatoes, and bell peppers with a blend of spices, resulting in a flavorful and aromatic dish. Pav Bhaji is typically garnished with chopped onions, fresh coriander, and a squeeze of lemon juice, making it a delicious and satisfying meal.


Ingredients


  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 1 cup chopped onions
  • 1 cup chopped tomatoes
  • 1 cup boiled and mashed potatoes
  • 1/2 cup boiled and mashed peas
  • 1/2 cup chopped bell peppers
  • 1 teaspoon ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 teaspoon red chili powder
  • 1 teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1 teaspoon pav bhaji masala
  • Salt to taste
  • 1/4 cup chopped fresh coriander leaves
  • Lemon wedges for garnish
  • Pav (bread rolls) for serving
